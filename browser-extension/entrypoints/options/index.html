What FeedBridge sees

Nothing runs until you click the toolbar icon. The page’s own feed links are read in your browser. FeedBridge’s server only gets a page’s address when you press Scan with FeedBridge, open the extension on YouTube or GitHub, or subscribe to a feed. Results are cached for up to a day so repeat lookups are quick. Pages you don’t ask about are never sent.
